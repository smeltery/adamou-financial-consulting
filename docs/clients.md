# For clients

Afin is Adamou Financial Consulting, Matt Adamou’s financial coaching practice.
Matt helps individuals, couples, and small businesses with personalized budgets,
debt payoff plans, and financial goals.

## Start a conversation

Choose **Find your next step** on the homepage, or open `/contact/`.
Provide your name, email, preferred contact method, who the coaching is for,
and what you would like to change. A phone number is required if you prefer a
phone call. Other context is optional.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Explore services] --> B[Share your goals]
  B --> C[Send your introduction]
  C --> D[Talk with Matt]
  D --> E[Agree on next steps]
```

If the button says **Prepare my introduction**, review the generated text and
choose **Open email draft**. Send that email to complete your inquiry. If your
email app does not open, copy the text and email it to Matt.

If the button says **Send my introduction**, wait for the confirmation that the
inquiry was sent. If delivery fails, your answers remain on the page and an
email draft is available. Navigating away or reloading may clear your answers.

## What to share

Goals, challenges, timing, and approximate context are enough. Please leave out
account numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, and financial documents.
There is no document upload in this initial introduction.
